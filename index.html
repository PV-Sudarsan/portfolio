How we engage

A clear path from first call to steady operations

Every engagement follows the same four stages, so you always know what has been done, what comes next and what you will receive at each step.

Stage 1

Assess

We map your assets, cloud accounts, code and current controls, then rank the risks by business impact.

Output: risk register
Stage 2

Design

Target architecture, security controls and a delivery roadmap agreed with your team.

Output: roadmap and architecture
Stage 3

Build

Our engineers implement pipelines, infrastructure, detections or product features in short sprints.

Output: working systems
Stage 4

Operate

The SOC and platform team run and improve what we built, with monthly reports you can share upward.

Output: SLAs and reports
